v0.5.29 - HUD shutdown and idle-update fix

• The external HUD now exits when its original Palworld process ends instead of surviving a fast restart with the analyzer disabled.
• Unchanged state files are no longer repeatedly parsed. Hidden HUDs perform fewer checks and skip rendering; repeated process-name scans and periodic topmost resets are removed.
• Visible HUD checks remain at 200 ms. Damage collection, skill attribution, and native F3 behavior are unchanged from v0.5.28.
• Local gameplay testing reported substantially less stutter; this is not a universal performance guarantee or a fix for unrelated UE4SS startup failures. UE4SS and PalSchema are not modified.
• Fully exit Palworld before updating, allow the older HUD to exit, then restart the game.

v0.5.29 - HUD 退出與閒置更新修正

• 外部 HUD 在原本的遊戲程序結束時退出，不再因快速重開而讓已停用分析器的舊 HUD 繼續存活。
• 狀態檔未變更時不再重複解析；隱藏時減少檢查並停止繪製，移除反覆按名稱查找程序及定期重設置頂。
• 顯示時仍維持 200 毫秒檢查頻率；傷害收集、技能歸屬與原生 F3 行為沿用 v0.5.28。
• 本機實戰回報卡頓明顯改善，但不保證所有硬體皆有相同效果，也不宣稱修復無關的 UE4SS 啟動失敗。本版不修改 UE4SS 或 PalSchema。
• 更新前請完整退出 Palworld，等舊版 HUD 結束後再重開。
