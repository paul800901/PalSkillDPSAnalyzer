v0.5.28 – Mummy Rush four-hit attribution

• Three full live Mummy Rush casts each produced four source-less native hits. The newest 2,126 + 2,111 + 1,813 + 2,108 capture showed that other resolved damage can advance the global native sequence between swings.
• The skill's displayed 500 power belongs to the complete four-hit cast, not to each individual hit.
• The exact equipped Action now binds at most four forward-sequence hits beginning at least two seconds into the cast. Global sequence gaps and missed middle swings are allowed; duplicate/backward sequences, fifth hits, and tails beyond 0.35 seconds remain unresolved.
• No damage amount participates in attribution.

v0.5.28－突襲木乃伊四段歸屬

• 實機三次完整突襲木乃伊都產生四筆無來源原生 Hit；最新一組 2,126＋2,111＋1,813＋2,108 證實其他已辨識傷害會在揮擊間推進全域原生序號。
• 面板威力 500 屬於整次四段施放，不是每一段各自 500。
• 精確配裝 Action 只會從開始至少 2 秒後承接最多四段遞增序號 Hit；允許全域序號跳號與中間揮空，重複／倒退序號、第五段或超過 0.35 秒尾段時仍維持未歸屬。
• 歸屬規則不使用傷害數字。
